# Design and Methods Paper | ELLA Research

## Community Participation in Community-Based Crime Prevention Experiences in Latin America and Africa

**Design and Methods Paper · 25 February 2015**

**Authors:** Ayobami Ojebode, Alan Melara, Ricardo Córdova, Víctor Tablas

**Institutions:** Fundación Dr. Guillermo Manuel Ungo (FUNDAUNGO), El Salvador; Department of Communication & Language Arts, University of Ibadan, Nigeria.

## Overview

This research design examines whether **direct and active community participation** contributes to the outcomes of community-based crime prevention (CBCP) programmes in Latin America and Africa.

The proposed methodology combines a review of existing evidence with **eight comparative case studies**: four in Latin America and four in Africa. The case selection strategy distinguishes typical and diverse experiences and compares direct versus indirect and active versus passive forms of participation.

## Research approach

- Literature and documentary evidence review.
- Mapping and classification of community-based crime prevention initiatives.
- Selection of typical and diverse cases in both regions.
- In-depth comparative qualitative case studies and expert interviews.
- Policy engagement and dissemination of findings.
